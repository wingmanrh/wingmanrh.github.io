How THICC works

Everything on this page comes from public data anyone can check. No accounts, no backend, no trust needed. Here is exactly what each number means.

ETH in pool

The main number. We read each pool's exact state from the chain (its price and its liquidity) and compute how much ETH the locked position holds right now. Buys push ETH in. Nobody can ever pull it out.

  • Read via StateView 0xF3334192D15450CdD385c8B70e03f9A6bD9E673b
  • Formula: amount0 over the curve range, ticks -160100…198050
  • Exact for standard launches. Legacy pools (pre Aug 5) are slightly undercounted.
  • Pool value shows both sides. The token half is the pool's own unsold supply at spot, so it moves with price, not with new money. We show it but never rank by it.

Fees relocked

Every trade pays a 0.25% fee. Most of it gets bought back into the locked pool, forever. This column counts every one of those events since the platform launched. It only ever goes up.

  • Event Claimed on the compounder 0xf9526Dd3…E99a
  • Full history scanned from block 22,754,669 (Jul 29)
  • A tiny share (~3% measured) pays the bots that trigger it

Backing (in the token view)

ETH in pool divided by FDV: how much of this token's market cap is real money locked under it. You'll find it when you open a token's row. We keep it out of the main table because small tokens naturally show higher percentages, so comparing it across tokens misleads.

  • Pure division, both inputs shown on this page
  • Never used for ranking or comparison

Why you can't fake it

Climbing this leaderboard has exactly one path: real ETH volume on your token. Wash trading "works", but every fake trade donates 0.15% of it into the locked pool. You can buy your rank, but the money stays locked in your pool for your holders. We are fine with that.
