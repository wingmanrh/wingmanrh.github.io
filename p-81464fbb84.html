Where the money comes from

Wingman takes nothing on founding deals. On every later deal it takes a 5% platform cut, collected on-chain inside the same splitter as everyone else's share.

5% of every paid deal

The platform's cut, collected by contract — visible to everyone, owed to no one's trust.

50% to the founders

Half of all platform revenue flows to the founding ten, split by what each founder brings.

10 seats, then closed

The ten seats are the whole cohort. Once they're filled, that's it — no fake countdown, no round two.

What a seat is worth

  • Your slice tracks what you bring. The pool is split among founders each quarter by the deals you promote and the deals you refer. Bring deals, earn more. Go quiet and your slice flows to the founders still building; show up again and it comes back. The seat is for life — the pay is earned continuously.
  • Nobody rides for free — including us. The pool always concentrates on whoever's still showing up. You can't be diluted by someone who vanished after week one. That's the point.
  • Paid on-chain, from public data. Every paid deal's 5% flows into one revenue contract. Each quarter's split is derived from the on-chain deal record — anyone can recompute it, so we can't quietly change the math. You withdraw your own slice; nobody can touch or block it.
  • You're long the whole platform, not one token. Every deal that clears through Wingman feeds the pool. You're not betting on a single launch — you're tolling all of them.

What we ask — and what we'll never do

  • Be a real reach partner. Take the deals that fit you, pass on the rest. No quota, no exclusivity, no upfront anything. Your existing rates stay yours — including on deals we bring you.
  • You're never listed. Your name stays private until you choose to attach it to a deal.
  • No bag-for-shill. No token allocation in exchange for promotion — your alignment is the revenue share, which keeps you clean and keeps this a trust brand. When you promote, you disclose.

How a seat gets confirmed

  1. We talk. If it's a fit, you're in the founding ten.
  2. When the cohort is full, we deploy the founder revenue contract with all ten addresses baked in — and hand you the address so you can verify your own seat.
  3. From then on, every paid deal feeds the pool, and each quarter it pays the founders by what they brought — you included, for as long as you're bringing.

No money ever moves from you. Worst case, Wingman goes nowhere and you've spent one conversation. Best case, you hold a standing claim on the fee layer of an entire chain — one that pays you exactly as much as you keep building it.
